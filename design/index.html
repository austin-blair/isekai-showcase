Project: Isekai Gacha

Design system

Object UI, version three.

Everything you touch in the game is a physical thing — a card, a book, a sealed letter, a ball under your thumb. Each one lit from the upper left, casting its own shadow, sitting apart from everything else.

There are no panels and no borders anywhere. The dark between things is what separates them. Three faces do all the reading, and gold never covers more than a hundredth of the screen.

The system is locked and versioned. What follows is the complete set.
